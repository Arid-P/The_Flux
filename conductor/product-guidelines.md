# Documentation & UI Guidelines

## Voice / Tone
Professional and technical, concise and direct with no buttering, flattery, or unnecessary compliments.

## Design Principles
- **Consistent UI/UX:** Enforces the strict flat 2D, zero‑blue color palette across Code1 (Flutter) and Code2 (Tailwind).  
- **Accessibility‑first:** Prioritizes core Android Accessibility Services required for app blocking and friction mechanics.  
- **Performance first:** Protects the offline‑first SQLite/Hive architecture and drift‑free delta arithmetic used in the focus timer.
